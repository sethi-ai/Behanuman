Learning terms & reflections

Traditional meaning is presented separately from Let’s reflect, which offers contemporary interpretations for personal contemplation. Reflections are not scripture, scientific findings, medical advice or promises of particular results.

Mark Learned records your own choice. The 21-day milestone celebrates time committed, not mastery of every verse. This test version’s detailed source and scripture review is still in progress.

Privacy

When you sign in with Google, Be Hanuman receives your basic profile, including name, email address and profile picture. It does not request access to your Gmail messages or contacts.

Your account stores your Sankalp, intention, promise, reading activity and learning progress in Convex to support saving and syncing. Account activity also contributes to usage counts. Hosting and sign-in providers may process technical request and session information to operate their services.

Without sign-in, practice is kept in this browser. Browser storage also holds preferences and cached account progress. Clearing it can remove unsynced work. Signing out does not delete your account.

To request account-data deletion, use Settings → Delete account. For privacy questions, contact sethi.mailbox1@gmail.com. Test-app data is separate from the live app.

Terms

Be Hanuman is a devotional reading and learning app. This is a test version: features and lesson content may change, and errors may occur.

Use the app for your personal practice. Traditional explanations and modern reflections are learning companions, not guarantees of spiritual, health or other outcomes. Choose intentions and promises that are appropriate for you.

Keep your Google account secure. Saving across devices requires sign-in and a working connection; check that saving has completed before switching devices. You can stop using the app or request account deletion in Settings.

Questions or corrections: sethi.mailbox1@gmail.com. Updated 16 October 2026.
