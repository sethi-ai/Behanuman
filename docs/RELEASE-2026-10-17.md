## What is available
Guests can enter Chant without personal setup or Google. Optional Sankalp, recitation choice, reading, Home, 43-verse/21-lesson Learn and saved Google-account practice remain available. Simple mode keeps only Chant, with Full mode at the top right. Learn retains Favourites, Recent and Most read; the bottom Revisit list is removed, while saved revisit marks and the per-verse action remain intact.

h.i is available to signed-in users after an explicit message/context transmission notice. The reviewed pilot uses Chaupai7 and one separately attributed Ramcharitmanas passage. Traditional meaning, story and AI-generated modern interpretation are separate. It does not speak as Hanuman or generate new scripture. Unapproved Valmiki samples and the full archive are not fallback generation sources. Test-only source approval does not establish wider commercial/edition clearance.

Claude uses the existing server-only test configuration. Guests/deleted accounts are blocked; sources, safety, parser, input/output and rate limits apply. Other account usage, earlier calls, taxes and hosting costs are outside that allocation. No raw concerns or generated replies are retained in app tables/logs; only fixed story history stays locally. The user chooses whether to send their message to Anthropic.
